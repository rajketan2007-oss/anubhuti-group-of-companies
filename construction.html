Methodology

Our Rigorous 6-Step Construction Process

We follow a standardized, milestone-driven framework that eliminates uncertainties, ensures regulatory compliance, and delivers unyielding structural strength.

01

Consultation

Initial site inspection, soil bearing capacity testing, and client requirement briefing.

02

Design & Plan

Architectural drafting, structural calculations, 3D BIM modeling, and BOQ estimation.

03

Approvals

Municipal sanctioning, environmental clearance, fire NOCs, and statutory legal sign-offs.

04

Construction

Precision civil execution, RCC framework, MEP installations, and masonry work.

05

Quality Checks

Multi-tier structural testing, cube compressive tests, and finishing snag list audits.

06

Handover

Delivery of occupancy certificate (OC), as-built drawings, and warranty dossier.

Materials & Engineering Quality Benchmarks

We partner solely with Tier-1 manufacturers. Every batch of cement, steel, and aggregates undergoes rigorous mandatory laboratory verification before site usage.

Fe 550D TMT Reinforcement Steel

Exclusive use of primary steel producers (Tata Tiscon, JSW, SAIL). Superior elongation properties guaranteeing seismic Zone 4/5 resilience and corrosion resistance.

53 Grade Ultratech / ACC Cement

High early-strength OPC 53 and PPC grades for specialized batching plant mixes. Daily 7-day and 28-day cube compression lab testing documented for client review.

Multi-Barrier Crystalline Waterproofing

Integral crystalline waterproofing admixtures applied to basements, sunken slabs, and terraces, backed by our comprehensive 10-year written non-leakage warranty.

High-Strength AAC Blocks

Autoclaved Aerated Concrete blocks providing high thermal insulation, reduced dead load on building frames, and superior acoustic sound attenuation.

FRLS Copper Wiring & CPVC Piping

Fire Retardant Low Smoke (FRLS) wiring from Polycab/Havells with Schneider circuit breakers, paired with Astral/Ashirvad lead-free CPVC plumbing systems.

3-Tier Independent QA/QC Audits

Internal quality inspectors, client PMC representatives, and third-party NABL-accredited civil testing agencies verify every structural stage.

Commercial buildings and multi-storey high-rises typically span 18 to 28 months. We include a milestone chart and penalty clauses for delays in our contract.
Yes. Our in-house liaison cell handles end-to-end statutory clearances, including local municipal corporation building plan approvals, structural engineer vetting, fire department NOCs, groundwater authority sanctions, electricity load sanctions, and state RERA filings.

Typical milestones include: Advance mobilization (10%), Completion of Plinth/Foundation (15%), Ground floor slab (10%), Successive slabs (30%), Brickwork and Plastering (15%), Plumbing & Electrical rough-ins (10%), and Final Handover with OC (10%). Clients only pay after a milestone is certified.
