How We Work

The Anubhuti 5-Stage Design Roadmap

A frictionless journey from initial concept sketches to walking into your move-in-ready, beautifully decorated space.

01

Consultation

In-depth discussion on lifestyle, design aspirations, budget, and site laser measurements.

02

Concept & Moodboard

Curating color palettes, material samples (veneers, laminates, fabrics), and floor plans.

03

3D Photorealistic Design

Detailed 3D renders with lighting, furniture, finishes, and final fixed-price quotation.

04

Factory Precision Execution

CNC factory manufacturing, modular assembly, civil work, electrical, and surface finishing.

05

White-Glove Handover

Deep cleaning, 140-point quality check, styling setup, and 10-year warranty card handover.
